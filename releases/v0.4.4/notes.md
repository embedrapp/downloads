## What’s New

- A new sign-in: one screen for signing in and creating an account, six-digit email codes instead of verification links, and a short first-run setup.
- Mechanical designs build on your Mac or PC. Embedr installs its CAD tools the first time you need them, and the cloud takes over while they install.
- The agent can keep working while a board routes or a mechanical model builds, and it can reroute just the nets you name.
- Claude models now have a 400k context.
- If you connect your ChatGPT or OpenCode plan, Embedr keeps using it. The model picker shows whose plan runs each model.
- Subagent threads show the model they run on.
- Electronics-only projects stay electronics only: the agent adds firmware only when you ask.
- Projects with more than one board keep a separate BOM for each board.
- Pico W, Pico 2 and other RP2040 boards build, and firmware builds work in folders with spaces in their names.
- Faster, more focused agent runs on large boards, with shorter tool results and accurate counts of unconnected items.
- Fixes for stray empty chats, chats lost on a full disk, sign-in on computers with a fast clock, and the Linux AppImage board tools.
