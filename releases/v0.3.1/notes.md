## What’s New

- A refreshed workspace. Switch between Agent and IDE views, find recent projects faster, and get started with guided project ideas.
- Simpler setup. Connect your ChatGPT or OpenCode account during onboarding, with clearer model choices and billing labels.
- Bring your KiCad projects into Embedr, review board checks, and explore parts in the new bill of materials workspace.
- Go from board to manufacturing. Preview your design, prepare fabrication files, and request PCBWay quotes from one guided workflow.
- Keep agent work moving with improved follow-up messages, editable chat titles, clearer activity, and richer completion notifications.
- Find targets, devices, branches, and keyboard shortcuts more easily with updated editor controls.
- Use more of your development workflow in the browser, with improved terminals, Git, firmware, PCB tools, and recovery after reconnecting.
- More reliable sign-in recovery, datasheet tools, project builds, and board exports.

PCB Auto Place and Auto Layout now require an active paid Embedr subscription, with plan guidance shown before you continue.

Windows users: install v0.3.1 manually to enable in-app downloads and installation for future updates.
