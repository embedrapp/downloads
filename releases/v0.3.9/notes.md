## What’s New

- Share a desktop project with a link, or only with the people you invite by email.
- The agent can hand work to helpers, such as a reviewer or a sourcing assistant, that run in their own threads and report back when they finish.
- Terminals and long-running commands keep going after the agent moves on, and the agent can follow them, answer their prompts and, with your approval, type into your open terminal.
- The Mechanical view is now a full review tool: CAD-style views and mouse controls, wireframe and section views, exploded views, notes you can pin to parts for the agent, blueprint sheets and step-by-step assembly guides.
- Kimi K3.1 Pro is available on Hobby and Pro plans.
- Files you paste into a request stay available to the agent in long conversations.
- Autorouting works on more real boards and always returns its best result, even on large boards.
- Windows commands with quoted paths now work, and interrupted Windows updates recover on their own.
- First firmware builds get more time, and the agent explains a timeout instead of trying the same build again.
- Clearer guidance when an Arduino build on Apple Silicon needs Rosetta.
- The Manufacturing tab keeps your progress when you switch away and back.
- More reliable agent runs when you close the window, and better handling of projects with more than one board.
