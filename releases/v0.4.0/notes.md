## What’s New

- Send the agent a message while it works and it picks it up at the next step, without stopping what it was doing.
- Queued messages stack above the composer, where you can edit, remove or send one straight into the current run. Choose how Enter behaves while the agent works in Settings.
- Git in the app now covers everyday work: discard changes, resolve merge conflicts, stash, undo the last commit, amend, sync with your remote and clone a repository. Version Control opens as its own tab with a clearer diff.
- Long datasheets index in the background, with no page limit, while the agent keeps working. Indexing continues after a restart, and datasheet links that open a viewer page now work.
- Autorouting handles more boards: it uses empty inner layers, routes parts with custom pad shapes, and connects pads to inner ground and power planes.
- The agent can flip parts to the other side of the board, and new parts land where your design places them.
- The board's 3D view opens faster and stays up to date as the board changes.
- Long agent runs keep going until the work is done, and time spent waiting for your answers no longer counts toward their limit.
- The agent respects choices you have already made, such as confirmed parts or manual routing, even in long conversations.
- Helpers the agent starts now have their own names, so you can tell them apart.
- More reliable on Linux, when the AI provider is busy, and when a firmware build was interrupted.
