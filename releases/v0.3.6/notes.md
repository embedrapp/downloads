## What’s New

- Claude Opus 5.5, Sonnet 5.5 and Haiku 5.5 now connect directly to Anthropic.
- Choose your board maker up front, and Embedr designs and checks the board to its manufacturing limits from the start.
- Route boards automatically in a single step, with small manufacturing issues fixed for you.
- Prepare PCBs, with or without assembly, for PCBWay from a redesigned Manufacturing tab, with quotes that match your board's size and thickness.
- Schematics arrange themselves into clear, readable blocks.
- Click a component to find it in both the schematic and the board, and send design-check findings straight to chat.
- A faster, more realistic 3D board view.
- Plan Mode now works as a short interview that produces a written spec you can implement in one click.
- Attach several images, Altium files and long pasted text to a message.
- Design mechanical parts and assemblies, and bring your board into them as a 3D model.
- Conversations recover after a reload, and long agent runs no longer stall.
